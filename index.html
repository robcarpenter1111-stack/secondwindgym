The gym behind the glass.

Premium without the attitude. Dedicated zones for strength, functional training, cardio and recovery, dark finishes and warm light, and a welcome that's the same on your first day as your five-hundredth.

01

Strength

Racks, platforms and a heavy lifting room. Panatta, Nautilus and Citadel machines, chosen one at a time.

02

Functional and cardio

A green turf loop for sleds, carries and sprints, wrapped around a timber platform of treadmills.

03

Recovery

Space to stretch and reset, then the café for a shake on the way out.

The equipment

Chosen one machine
at a time.

No package deal from one catalogue. Sergio is picking every piece on the floor himself, from the three makers he'd put his own name to.

Italy

Panatta

Built in Apiro since 1963. The strength machines the best gyms in Europe are furnished with, made to order for this floor.

United States

Nautilus

The name that invented the modern strength machine. Selectorised pieces that feel right from the first rep.

Queensland

Citadel Strength

Plate-loaded machines built by two brothers in Gympie from Australian steel, with a twenty-year warranty. Pendulum squat, hack squat, hip press.

Questions

Before you ask

When does Second Wind open?+

In 2027. The founding list hears the exact date first, gets an invite to the café opening, and founding members get a preview session in the gym.

Where is it?+

The ground floor of 220 St Georges Terrace, in the Perth CBD, a short walk from Brookfield Place. The café opens onto the street.

I haven't trained in years. Is it for me?+

Yes. The gym is designed to be as welcoming for new members as for regulars, and you start from where you are.

How much is membership?+

Pricing goes to the founding list first.

Do I have to pay to join the list?+

No. Joining the founding list is free and commits you to nothing. You'll be offered a founding membership before it opens to anyone else.

Can I use the café without a membership?+

Yes. The café is open to everyone: come in for a coffee, a shake or something to eat, member or not.
