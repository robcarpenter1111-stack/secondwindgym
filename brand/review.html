My favourite

Two strokes, two weights

Of the first fourteen, two logos survive every test: the two strokes and the wing. Both have a real symbol that says something about the name, both hold up on glass and cups, and both gain from a bolder weight in the gym. Everything else either has no icon, or turns into "SW", which could be any gym in Perth.

My favourite is the two strokes, light in the café and bold in the gym. It is the quieter of the two, which suits the room HUB has drawn, and it is the only one of the first fourteen that is as much about the café as the gym: two strokes read as calm on a coffee cup and as effort on a gym wall. The wing is the strong alternative if you want more energy, and it would be a good choice too.
