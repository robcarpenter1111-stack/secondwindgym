Second Wind Athletic Club · logo review · for Sergio

Fourteen logos,
one test

Every logo we explored, put through the same test: the full mark, four weights, the icon it becomes when it's small, and how it behaved on the real walls, glass and cups of HUB's design. The same format for each, so the only thing that changes is the logo.

How to read a spread: the hero is the logo at its best. The four weights are the same letters, thicker. The three squares are the icon at profile-picture size on linen, charcoal and walnut.

Our recommendation

Two strokes, two weights

Of the fourteen, two logos survive every test: the two strokes and the wing. Both have a real symbol that says something about the name, both hold up on glass and cups, and both gain from a bolder weight in the gym. Everything else either has no icon, or turns into "SW", which could be any gym in Perth.

We recommend the two strokes, light in the café and bold in the gym. It is the quieter of the two, which suits the room HUB has drawn, and it is the only one of the fourteen that is as much about the café as the gym: two strokes read as calm on a coffee cup and as effort on a gym wall. The wing is the strong alternative if you want more energy, and it would be a good choice too.
