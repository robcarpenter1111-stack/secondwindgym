01The idea

Second Wind Athletic Club is a premium café and training club in the Perth CBD: a public café as the front door, warm and refined in the room, serious on the floor behind the glass.

HUB Interiors' design sets the tone: walnut, bronze, concrete and warm light, curves instead of hard edges. The identity belongs to that room. The training is the real thing and the welcome is warm. The entry criterion is effort, not ability.

A second wind is the turn that arrives after the point you thought you were finished. It lives in the identity as the symbol: two strokes, the first short, a gap, the second longer. It is never spelled out in motivational copy.

Feels like

Warm, refined, confident, direct, athletic, considered, crafted, grown up, alive.

Never

Shouty, laddish, corporate, sentimental, motivational-poster, precious. Not a chain gym, not a nightclub, not a supplement brand.

Who is in the room

CBD professionals and serious lifters, together, by design. Credible to the strongest person in the room, welcoming to the newest.

Reproduction notes

  • Print: linen as an uncoated warm white stock, not printed. Walnut in CMYK or Pantone; proof under warm light. Bronze as foil rather than a flat ink.
  • Embroidery: thread matched to the swatches on a stitched sample. Thread reads darker than screen.
  • Screen print: charcoal or walnut on linen fabric; linen ink on walnut or charcoal fabric needs an underbase.
  • Glass: frosted etch-effect film only. No coloured film on glass.
  • Signage at night: warm white, 3000 K, as HUB's lighting. Never cool white, never RGB.

06Tone of voice

The voice already exists. It is Sergio's. Direct, unsentimental, standards stated plainly rather than enforced. Plain verbs. No filler. Encouraging without cheerleading. Slightly masculine in tone, and every woman, beginner and older member must feel entirely comfortable reading it.

Short sentences

Under 15 words where possible. One idea each. Full stops, not exclamation marks.

Specific over hype

"Three sessions a week, 50 minutes, coached" beats "unleash your potential". Numbers, names, times.

We, not you

"We choose to be here." Coaches and members together. Never "you will" or "you need to".

07Photography and art direction

Reference is MAAP: athletes look serious and human rather than posed. Natural or warm light, real members, the actual room. Documenting the work, not performing it.

Do
  • Warm, available light; 3000 K interiors as they are
  • Real members and coaches, mid-set, between sets, chalk on hands
  • Walnut, bronze, concrete and tan upholstery in frame as the palette
  • The build: shell, flooring, deliveries, first session, in order
  • Portraits straight to camera, calm, no flex
  • Detail: knurling, a plate, the cut on a cap
Don't
  • Blue or cool grading, neon, lens flare, HDR
  • Stock models, posed flexing, sweat spray
  • Dark-for-premium: crushed blacks, moody vignettes
  • Motivational text over photos
  • Mirror selfies as brand content
  • Anyone looking intimidated or intimidating

08Devices and motifs

The gap
Two strokes · the pause · 1 : 1.95
Tonal patternKnit, emboss, frost. Never printed loud
The rule

31.9531° S · 115.8533° E

No. 07 / 50

Bronze rule, coordinates, numbering
  • The gap between the two strokes is the brand's only ornament: the pause before the second wind. It appears in the symbol and as the pause in motion. The strokes are never a stripe, a swoosh or a wing.
  • The tonal pattern is the bar letterforms sliced by the cut, used tonally only: knitted, embossed, frosted. It is never printed in a contrasting colour.
  • The bronze rule is a short horizontal line, 14 to 30% of the width, placed above a label. One per layout.
  • Coordinates and numbering locate things: the door, the founding tees, the member card. Set in DM Mono.

See the Range and Lookbook pages.
