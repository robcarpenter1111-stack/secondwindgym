Our recommendation

Sergio, you've seen everything you asked for on the other page. Here's one way we think it could all come together nicely: one system for the club and the café, built from your notes, with a few small refinements we've explained below. It's a suggestion to sit alongside your favourites, not instead of them. It's your brand and your call, so please let us know what you think.

Why we think it works

It keeps everything you liked: the two strokes, bold and leaning for movement, a little more extended for presence, the cut tying the letters to the icon, and the serif giving the café its own calm voice.

Two rules that keep it premium

The serif is for the café's name only; menus, prices and signage text stay in the brand's sans. And the lean lives in the logo only; headings and the website stay upright. That discipline is what makes it feel expensive rather than busy.

The café name

First Wind Café is in as your working name. The one-stroke idea works with any name, so we can settle it together; before it goes on a cup or a sign we'll check nobody nearby already trades under it.

A refinement we'd suggest: on 01 and the variations you asked for, ATHLETIC CLUB was stretched to the full width of the name, which spaced the letters so far apart they were hard to read and too thin to light. It now leans with the letters and finishes flush under WIND, so the strokes open the mark and the club line closes it. It's a small change, but it's the difference between a logo and a well-drawn logo.
